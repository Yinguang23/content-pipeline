CONTENT PIPELINE — ENGLISH IDEA TO CHINESE REDNOTE PACKAGE

PURPOSE

This workflow takes every newly created English video idea from my English Caption/Codex workflow and automatically turns it into a complete Chinese content package for RedNote.

The workflow should only process NEW ideas that have not already been completed.

==================================================
SOURCE FORMAT
==================================================

Every new English video idea should be stored in a consistent format like this:

TAB: [number]
STATUS: NEW
TOPIC: [topic]
CORE IDEA: [one-sentence explanation]
ENGLISH TITLE: [title]
ENGLISH CAPTION: [caption]
NOTES: [optional supporting notes]

Example:

TAB: 65
STATUS: NEW
TOPIC: Choice overload
CORE IDEA: Too many options can make people less likely to buy.
ENGLISH TITLE: More Choices, Fewer Sales
ENGLISH CAPTION: Giving customers more options can sometimes make them less likely to choose anything.
NOTES: Relevant to consumer psychology and retail.

==================================================
MAIN PROCESSING RULE
==================================================

Whenever a new entry with STATUS: NEW appears, process that idea automatically.

Do NOT ask me to repeat these instructions.

Do NOT regenerate previously processed tabs.

Do NOT process entries already marked as PROCESSED.

For each new video idea, complete the following steps.

==================================================
STEP 1 — FIND THE REAL CORE IDEA
==================================================

Identify what the video is ACTUALLY about.

Do not simply repeat the academic term.

Ask internally:

- What is the surprising thing happening?
- What two things seem like they should not go together?
- What is the strongest consequence?
- What is the most concrete object, company, number, behavior, or situation in the story?
- What would make someone stop scrolling and think:
  “Wait, how does that make sense?”

The title should usually sell the mystery or consequence.

The body of the video explains the concept.

==================================================
STEP 2 — TITLE PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Use this principle:

DO NOT TITLE THE LESSON.
TITLE THE STRANGEST CONSEQUENCE OF THE LESSON.

Avoid leading with academic terminology unless it genuinely improves curiosity.

BAD:
错误共识效应
知识诅咒
选择过载
感官心理学

BETTER:
你的朋友不是所有人
为什么专家反而不会教？
选择越多，买得越少？
声音为什么能改变味道？

Prefer:

- contradiction
- surprising cause and effect
- recognizable brands
- familiar objects
- money
- numbers
- everyday habits
- extreme consequences
- strange relationships
- provocative questions
- “why does X cause Y?”
- “who invented X?”
- “how did X become Y?”
- “why does losing feel like winning?”
- “how do meatballs sell furniture?”

Avoid:

- vague motivational language
- overly philosophical titles
- academic-jargon-first titles
- generic “你知道吗”
- generic “心理学告诉你”
- titles that explain everything immediately
- clickbait that is not supported by the actual script

==================================================
STEP 3 — GENERATE 20 CHINESE TITLES
==================================================

For every new video idea, generate exactly 20 different Chinese title options.

Titles should:

- be concise
- sound natural in Chinese
- fit RedNote/social distribution
- create curiosity immediately
- use concrete nouns where possible
- emphasize contradiction or unexpected consequence
- avoid unnecessary explanation
- generally stay short enough to understand at a glance
- NOT be forced into exactly four Chinese characters
- NOT be forced into exactly four words
- usually aim for a short 3–10 word-style structure where natural

Use variety.

Possible title structures include:

为什么X反而Y？
X怎么卖出Y？
是谁发明了X？
X真的会导致Y？
越X，反而越Y？
你以为X，其实Y
X根本不是你想的那样
一个X改变了Y
X为什么能控制Y？
你看到的X可能是假的
X是怎么变成习惯的？
为什么X会让你继续？
X如何改变你的判断？
谁把X变成了规则？
为什么大家都以为X？

Do NOT make all 20 titles structurally identical.

==================================================
STEP 4 — SELECT THE STRONGEST TITLES
==================================================

After generating the 20 titles, select the strongest 3.

Label them:

最强标题 1
最强标题 2
最强标题 3

Do not rank them using numerical scores.

Choose based on:

- strongest curiosity gap
- clearest contradiction
- easiest to understand instantly
- strongest concrete imagery
- strongest connection to the actual story
- best fit with my historical high-performing title patterns

My past stronger title patterns include ideas like:

越精通越穷？
食物50%的口感全靠听觉
为什么聪明人需要3套人生？
越厉害的导师，越容易带废新手
靠1个指标，做到巨大商业结果
全球大量公司为什么做同一件事？

The common pattern is:

CONCRETE SUBJECT
+
UNEXPECTED RELATIONSHIP
+
CURIOSITY GAP

==================================================
STEP 5 — WRITE A LONG CHINESE REDNOTE CAPTION
==================================================

For each new video, write one substantial paragraph-style Chinese caption for RedNote.

The caption should be MUCH longer than a normal one-line social caption.

Target roughly 180–350 Chinese characters when the idea supports it.

It should read like a polished RedNote post, not a transcript.

Preferred structure:

1. Start with the surprising observation or question.
2. Make the situation feel familiar.
3. Explain the mechanism in simple language.
4. Add the broader implication.
5. End with a thought-provoking conclusion, tension, or open reflection.

Do NOT reveal the entire answer in the first sentence.

The first sentence should increase curiosity.

The caption should:

- sound conversational
- be intelligent but easy to read
- avoid excessive academic jargon
- explain the idea clearly
- preserve nuance
- avoid unsupported claims
- avoid fake statistics
- avoid fake certainty
- avoid sounding like an advertisement
- be written fully in Chinese
- use paragraph-style prose, not bullet points

The caption should help someone who watches the video understand why the idea matters.

==================================================
STEP 6 — HASHTAGS
==================================================

Provide a RedNote hashtag set after the caption.

Use approximately 5–8 hashtags.

Use a mix of:

- broad discovery category
- specific topic
- psychology/business/history/behavior term when relevant
- recognizable brand/topic keyword
- one or two general knowledge/discovery tags

Examples:

#心理学
#消费心理
#营销
#商业思维
#认知偏差
#冷知识
#品牌营销
#行为经济学

Do not spam unrelated hashtags.

STEP 8 — DUPLICATE PREVENTION
==================================================

Each TAB number is a unique content ID.

Before processing:

- if yes, skip it
- if no, process it

Never regenerate an existing tab unless I explicitly request a revision.

Maintain the tab number throughout the entire pipeline.

==================================================

Whenever the English Caption workflow creates a new completed idea:

The workflow should require no manual rewriting of these instructions.

FINAL QUALITY CHECK
==================================================

Before saving the output, verify:

- Is the core idea correct?
- Are there exactly 20 Chinese titles?
- Do the titles vary meaningfully?
- Do they use concrete hooks instead of academic labels?
- Are the strongest 3 selected?
- Is the RedNote caption substantial and paragraph-style?
- Is the caption fully in Chinese?
- Are the hashtags relevant?
- Is the TAB preserved?
- Has this TAB already been processed?
- Is STATUS changed to PROCESSED?
- Did the workflow avoid regenerating old entries?

If all checks pass, save and commit the output.
